# ficsit.app listing for Pogo Stick 1.0.0

## Short description (listing, max ~200 characters)

An equippable Pogo Stick: charge it, push off the surface you're looking at, and launch the opposite way. Bounces on hard landings. Fully configurable.

## Full description (mod page, Markdown)

Bounce around your factory on a **Pogo Stick**.

### How it works

- **Unlock:** comes with the **Jump Pads** milestone (Tier 2), next to the Jump Pad itself.
- **Craft:** in the Equipment Workshop from 10 Iron Rods, 5 Iron Plates and 25 Screws.
- **Charge:** equip it and hold **Primary Fire**. The bar fills over 2 seconds and the spring squashes.
- **Launch:** the stick pushes off the surface you're looking at and sends you the opposite way. Look down to go up, look at a wall to fly away from it. It only works when that surface is within reach, so looking at the sky does nothing.
- **Push-off marker:** a marker on your HUD shows where the stick would push off, green when it's in reach and red when it's too far, with the distance.
- **Bounce:** land hard while looking at the ground and you bounce back up instead of taking damage. Hold Primary Fire or crouch as you land to stop.
- **No fall damage** between a launch and your next landing.
- Plays the Jump Pad launch sound.

### Settings (Mods > Pogo Stick, changes apply immediately)

Charge time, launch strength, push-off range, cooldown, keep momentum, launch when fully charged, no fall damage, spring squash, bounce on landing, bounciness, minimum bounce speed, show push-off point, show push-off distance, and an advanced section for the stick's size and hold position.

### Known limitations

- **Tested in single player only.** Multiplayer and dedicated servers are untested. The launch is sent to the server, but problems may only show up for clients.
- Only you hear the launch sound. Other players don't.
- In third person the character holds the stick with a one-handed pose, because the two-handed pose distorts the character model for tools added by mods.
